Worttag supplemental wordbooks v1 — data attribution
=====================================================

Scope and changes
-----------------
These notices apply to the supplemental JSON resources in this directory, not
to Worttag's application code or its original curated cards. Worttag filtered,
normalised, deduplicated and reclassified the source material; corrected noun
gender and inflection; selected senses against examples; generated additional
examples where needed; and added Simplified Chinese translations. The CEFR
bands are Worttag course classifications and are not official Goethe word
lists. The source selection and ordering of Goethe exam lists were not copied.
